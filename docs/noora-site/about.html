    Our story

    From one small shop to a growing community

    NOORA began as a small collection of carefully made panjabi for friends and family. People noticed the fabric, the fit and the finishing, and the circle kept growing.

    Today we design everything from everyday tees to winter jackets, attar and complete outfit combos. Every piece follows the same rule: premium quality, modest design and a fair price.
